# Iran 1: Political, Legal, and Geopolitical Developments (2010–2018)

## Overview
Between January 2010 and November 2018, the Islamic Republic of Iran experienced a shift from intense international isolation to a brief period of diplomatic reintegration before returning to severe economic pressure. This era included the transition from the presidency of [Mahmoud Ahmadinejad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmoud_Ahmadinejad) to the administration of [Hassan Rouhani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassan_Rouhani), the signing and subsequent collapse of the [JCPOA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Comprehensive_Plan_of_Action) nuclear deal, and a fundamental realignment of Iran's regional influence across the Middle East.

---

## Timeline of Key Events

### 2010
**January 2010 – Assassination of Masoud Alimohammadi** The bombing of a senior nuclear scientist in Tehran marked the start of a multi-year campaign against Iran's nuclear program.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masoud_Alimohammadi

**June 2010 – UN Security Council Resolution 1929** The UN passed a severe round of sanctions targeting the IRGC, Iranian banking, and heavy weaponry imports.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_1929

**June 2010 – Stuxnet Discovery** The identification of the Stuxnet worm revealed the first major instance of physical infrastructure sabotage via digital means against the Natanz facility.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuxnet

**December 2010 – Subsidy Reform Implementation** Iran began replacing its massive state subsidies on energy and bread with a system of direct monthly cash transfers to citizens.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_subsidy_reform_plan

---

### 2011
**February 2011 – House Arrest of Opposition Leaders** Figures Mir-Hossein Mousavi and Mehdi Karroubi were detained following calls for protests, effectively neutralizing the Green Movement.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mir-Hossein_Mousavi

**November 2011 – Attack on the British Embassy** Protesters stormed the UK's diplomatic mission in Tehran, leading to a total severance of diplomatic ties with London for several years.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_attack_on_the_British_Embassy_in_Iran

---

### 2012
**January 2012 – Fordow Enrichment Launch** The IAEA confirmed that Iran had started enriching uranium to 20% purity at its fortified, underground Fordow facility.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fordow_Fuel_Enrichment_Plant

**July 2012 – EU Oil Embargo** The European Union prohibited the purchase and transport of Iranian crude oil, severely limiting the state's access to foreign currency.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctions_against_Iran

**October 2012 – Rial Currency Crisis** The Iranian Rial lost 40% of its value in a single week due to sanctions and inflation, sparking rare economic protests in Tehran's Grand Bazaar.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Rial

---

### 2013
**June 2013 – Election of Hassan Rouhani** Hassan Rouhani won the presidency on a platform of "constructive engagement" with the West and domestic economic revitalization.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Iranian_presidential_election

**November 2013 – Geneva Interim Agreement** Iran and the P5+1 nations signed a preliminary six-month deal to freeze nuclear progress in exchange for limited sanctions relief.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_interim_agreement_on_the_Iranian_nuclear_program

---

### 2014
**June 2014 – Intervention in Iraq** Iran became the first nation to deploy military advisors and hardware to help the Iraqi government defend Baghdad against the Islamic State (ISIS).  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_intervention_in_Iraq_(2014–present)

**December 2014 – OPEC Oil Price Slump** A global crash in oil prices placed significant fiscal strain on the Iranian budget during the final stages of nuclear negotiations.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010s_oil_glut

---

### 2015
**July 2015 – Signing of the JCPOA** Iran and world powers finalized a comprehensive nuclear deal intended to limit Iran’s nuclear capacity for over a decade.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Comprehensive_Plan_of_Action

**September 2015 – Mina Stampede Crisis** The deaths of hundreds of Iranian pilgrims during the Hajj in Saudi Arabia led to a major diplomatic rift between Tehran and Riyadh.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015_Mina_stampede

**September 2015 – Syrian Intervention Escalation** Iran significantly increased its military footprint in Syria to support the government, coordinating with the start of Russian air operations.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_involvement_in_the_Syrian_civil_war

---

### 2016
**January 2016 – Nuclear Implementation Day** The IAEA verified Iran's compliance with the JCPOA, leading to the official lifting of international nuclear-related sanctions.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Comprehensive_Plan_of_Action#Implementation

**January 2016 – Severing of Saudi Relations** Saudi Arabia cut all diplomatic ties after its embassy in Tehran was attacked following the execution of a prominent Shia cleric.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_attack_on_the_Saudi_diplomatic_missions_in_Iran

**April 2016 – Arrest of Dual Nationals** The detention of several high-profile dual-nationals began a period of "hostage diplomacy" that complicated renewed ties with the West.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_foreign_nationals_detained_in_Iran

---

### 2017
**January 2017 – Death of Hashemi Rafsanjani** The passing of the former president and influential centrist removed a key mediator between the state's hardline and reformist factions.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akbar_Hashemi_Rafsanjani

**May 2017 – Re-election of Hassan Rouhani** Rouhani secured a second term with a decisive popular mandate to continue his policies of economic and diplomatic opening.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Iranian_presidential_election

**June 2017 – ISIS Attacks in Tehran** Coordinated terrorist attacks on the Iranian Parliament and the Khomeini Mausoleum marked the first major ISIS operation on Iranian soil.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017_Tehran_attacks

**December 2017 – Nationwide Economic Protests** Protests erupted in Mashhad over high prices and corruption, quickly spreading to over 80 cities and taking on a political character.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017–2018_Iranian_protests

---

### 2018
**May 2018 – US Withdrawal from the JCPOA** The United States unilaterally exited the nuclear deal and initiated a "Maximum Pressure" campaign targeting the Iranian economy.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_withdrawal_from_the_Joint_Comprehensive_Plan_of_Action

**August 2018 – Reimposition of First Sanctions** The US restored sanctions on Iran's trade in gold, precious metals, the automotive sector, and its access to US banknotes.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctions_against_Iran

**September 2018 – Ahvaz Military Parade Attack** Gunmen opened fire on a military parade in Ahvaz, leading to dozens of casualties and an escalation of tensions with regional neighbors.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahvaz_military_parade_attack

**November 2018 – Full Energy and Banking Sanctions** The US reimposed its most critical sanctions on Iran's oil exports and central banking, finalizing the return to international isolation.  
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctions_against_Iran#2018_reimposition
